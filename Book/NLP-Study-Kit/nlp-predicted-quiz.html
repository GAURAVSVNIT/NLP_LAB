Q102 marks

DFA construction

Draw a DFA over {a,b} that accepts exactly the strings containing the substring "aab".

Q={q0,q1,q2,q3(final)}, Σ={a,b}
q0 --a--> q1 --a--> q2 --b--> q3(final, absorbing on a,b)
q1 --b--> q0  (fail back to start, saw a single a then b)
q2 --a--> q2  (stay — still just seen a's, waiting for the b)
q0 --b--> q0

Classic substring-recognizer construction: states track "how much of the target string aab have I matched so far," with a final absorbing accept state once the full substring is seen.

Good-Turing, multi-step

Given frequency-of-frequencies N₁=8, N₂=4, N₃=2, N₄=1 (and c=4 is trusted as-is, per Katz's threshold rule). Find c*₁, c*₂, c*₃, total token count N, and PGT(unseen).

Q102 marks

BPE merge steps

Corpus (word: frequency): low(5), lower(2), newest(6), widest(3). Character-split each word and apply 3 rounds of BPE merges. List the merges in order.

Init: l-o-w, l-o-w-e-r, n-e-w-e-s-t, w-i-d-e-s-t

Round 1: pair (e,s) occurs in newest(6) + widest(3) = 9 times — most frequent → merge "es"
Round 2: pair (es,t) occurs in newest(6) + widest(3) = 9 times → merge "est"
